# Pricing

<Note>
**Outline (Doc-D4).** Public dollar amounts, trial length, and full feature matrices will be published when the commercial plan freezes. Do not treat legacy third-party or pre-rebrand price lists as current.
</Note>

## How billing works

- Subscriptions and checkout are processed by **Creem**
- AdCue stores subscription state for access control after webhooks
- Manage billing from [Usage & Billing](https://adcue.app/app) in the app shell

Details (outline): [Usage & Billing](/product/usage-billing).

## Plan families

Product code currently models these plan codes:

| Plan | Intent |
|------|--------|
| **Free** | Get started — connect, explore, limited usage |
| **Plus** | Paid tier for higher tool / usage limits |
| **Pro** | Higher paid tier |
| **Max** | Highest paid tier |

Exact tool-call limits, seat rules, and list prices are **not** finalized on this page. When published, they will appear here and on the marketing pricing surface.

## What to expect at checkout

1. Sign in and open [adcue.app/app](https://adcue.app/app)
2. Choose a paid plan when upgrade buttons are enabled
3. Complete Creem Checkout
4. Return to AdCue; subscription status updates after webhook processing

## FAQ pointers

- Cancel / refunds / payment methods: [FAQ — billing](/knowledge/faq#billing--plans)
- Security of payment data: handled by Creem; AdCue stores subscription identifiers, not full card PANs
