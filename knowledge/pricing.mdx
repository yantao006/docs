# Pricing

AdCue has three paid plans.
Every plan includes a 7-day free trial.
There is no Free, Plus, or Max subscription plan.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Google Ads accounts | Tool calls | Monitoring | Support |
|------|-------|---------------------|------------|------------|---------|
| **Pro** | **$79/month** | 1 active account | 500/month | Daily | Email and community |
| **Business** | **$249/month** | Up to 5 active accounts | 2,000/month | Every 4 hours | Priority |
| **Enterprise** | **$799/month** | Unlimited | 10,000/month | Hourly | Dedicated |

Business is the recommended plan for teams managing multiple accounts.

## Included capabilities

### Pro

- 7-day free trial
- MCP tools for Google Ads
- AI clients including ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Codex, and OpenClaw
- Campaign management
- Performance dashboards
- Daily monitoring
- Email and community support

### Business

Business includes everything in Pro, plus:

- Up to 5 active Google Ads accounts
- 2,000 tool calls per month
- Multi-account management
- Cross-account and batch audit views
- Priority support
- Monitoring every 4 hours

### Enterprise

Enterprise includes everything in Business, plus:

- Unlimited Google Ads accounts
- 10,000 tool calls per month
- Dedicated support
- Hourly monitoring

## Platform availability

Google Ads is the live advertising platform in the current product.
Meta Ads is on the roadmap and is not included as a live capability.
White-label functionality is not currently productized.

## How billing works

Subscriptions and checkout are processed by **Creem**.
AdCue stores webhook-synced subscription state for access control.
Manage billing from [Usage & Billing](https://adcue.app/app) in the app shell.

If checkout is not configured for a deployment, AdCue fails closed and does not invent trial or paid access.

## Start a trial

1. Sign in and open [adcue.app/app](https://adcue.app/app).
2. Choose Pro, Business, or Enterprise.
3. Complete Creem Checkout.
4. Return to AdCue after checkout.
5. Wait for the webhook-synced status to show **Trial** or **Active** before using paid tools.
